Four days of community outreach, fellowship, and gospel witness.

What is this weekend?

A clear gospel witness for Star Valley

Star Valley Faith & Family Weekend is a multi-day outreach effort designed to place a clear, public gospel witness before our valley. Each gathering is built around a specific audience and schedule so families, men, ladies, students, and churches can participate in a natural way.

Our desire is not merely to host community events, but to use each gathering as an opportunity to point people plainly to Jesus Christ.

Weekend schedule

Event details

Here are the basic who, what, where, and when details for each gathering. Everything except the Sunday worship service requires a ticket. Tickets are free for guests and will be available for pickup beginning September 2.

Thursday, Sept. 10 · 7–9 PM

Ladies’ Night Out

Who: Ladies ages 15+

What: Simple craft, food, and fellowship

Where: Thayne Community Center

Ticket: Required · free for guests · available beginning Sept. 2

Friday, Sept. 11 · 7–9 PM

Men’s Steak Out

Who: Men ages 15+

What: Friendly shotgun and rifle shooting competition

Where: Silver Spring Lane in Thayne

Ticket: Required · free for guests · available beginning Sept. 2

Saturday, Sept. 12 · 10 AM–1 PM

Back to School Bash

Who: Teens ages 12–19, or entering grades 7–12 this fall

What: Games, pizza, and fellowship

Where: Thayne Unity Pavilion

Ticket: Required · free for guests · available beginning Sept. 2

Sunday, Sept. 13 · 11 AM

Star Valley Worship Service

Who: Everyone is invited

What: A valleywide worship service focused on Christ, Scripture, and the gospel

Where: Location to be announced

Ticket: No ticket required

Tickets are required for Ladies’ Night Out, Men’s Steak Out, and Back to School Bash. Tickets are free for guests and will be available for pickup beginning September 2.
